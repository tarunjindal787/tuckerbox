## Direction contract

THESIS: A roadside motor inn's website should feel like a well-kept paddock gate you're glad to pull into after a long highway stretch — not a resort fantasy, not a booking-engine template of stacked cards and a hero banner with a search widget bolted on. This surface refuses the generic-hotel-template default (dense rate-search widget crammed into the hero, uniform icon-plus-heading-plus-text amenity tiles, a carousel of interchangeable stock photos) and the flashy-resort default (gold accents, cursive script, spa language), neither of which is true to a practical, honest roadhouse motel.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is warm ivory (#faf6ee) with sandstone (#e7d9c3) banding between sections — never stark white. Forest green (#2f4538) carries the header, footer, and primary buttons as a committed dark field, not an accent dab. Charcoal (#2b2a27) for body text and line art. Clay (#b2593a) is reserved for exactly one job per view: the single primary call-to-action and small active-state marks. Muted eucalyptus (#8fa396) tints secondary surfaces and hover states. Headings set in Fraunces (editorial serif, generous optical size, warm italic for a handful of standalone pull-quotes/captions only — never as a label riding above a heading); body and UI in Inter. Corners are gently rounded (10–18px), never pill-everything; dividers are thin hairline rules, not boxed cards with heavy shadows. Photography (placeholder-labelled for now) is full-bleed and uncropped-feeling — the motel's actual building and rooms are the hero, not illustration or iconography. Room listings read as an editorial alternating photo/detail sequence (not a uniform grid of identical shadowed cards); facilities read as a dense, varied two-column annotated list grouped by theme (arrival & parking, in-room comfort, outdoors) rather than a tile grid of icon-over-heading-over-text.

STORY: A driver on the Hume Highway lands, instantly reads "Tuckerbox Motor Inn — South Gundagai," sees the real building, and has two unmissable next moves: check availability (→ Booking.com) or call now. Scrolling down answers, in order, the questions a tired traveller actually has: what are the rooms, what's included, what does it look like, when can I check in, where exactly is it, how do I get there. No section invents a reason to exist; the page ends at a confident, repeated contact/booking block, not a generic newsletter sign-up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed placeholder hero photograph of the motel exterior at ~72vh, forest-green header bar overlaid at the top (logotype left, nav + phone number right, mobile: hamburger). A sandstone content panel anchored bottom-left over the hero holds, in reading order: H1 "Tuckerbox Motor Inn" in large Fraunces, a single flowing subhead sentence naming South Gundagai and the Hume Highway position, and two buttons side by side — clay-filled primary "Check Availability" (→ Booking.com) and outlined secondary "View Rooms" (→ accommodation.html). No eyebrow/kicker label, no search widget, no autoplay carousel, no intro animation; the hero photo and both buttons are visible with zero scrolling and zero wait at every width down to 320px.

FORM: Direction is explicitly pinned by the client brief (business name, address, palette, typography family choices, "genuine photography as the main visual differentiator", and the named direction "The Modern Australian Country Roadside Retreat"); no concept-seed roll was run. seed: none (brief-pinned, no roll)
