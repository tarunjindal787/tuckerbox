A practical, comfortable stopover

Tuckerbox Motor Inn offers six room types to suit solo travellers, couples and families, from a Deluxe Queen Room through to a Two-Bedroom Suite. Every room includes a kitchenette, tea/coffee maker and free WiFi, so you can settle in quickly after a long drive.

Reception is staffed from 10am to 8pm; check-in runs 2pm–8pm and check-out is by 10am. Call ahead on 02 6944 0300 if you're arriving outside these hours.
