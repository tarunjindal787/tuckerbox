Frequently asked questions

Answers the property itself has given guests, sourced from the official site. Can't find yours here? Call reception on 02 6944 0300.

Is breakfast included, or is there a restaurant on site?

No. There is no restaurant at Tuckerbox Motor Inn and breakfast is not included. If you have specific meal needs, call reception on 02 6944 0300 to ask what's currently available nearby or on request.

Can I bring my pet?

No — Tuckerbox Motor Inn is not pet-friendly.

Do you have cots for babies or young children?

Yes. Porta cots can be set up ready for your arrival for an additional cost — ask reception when you book.

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2pm to 8pm, and check-out is by 10am. Reception is staffed 10am–8pm daily. If you need to request an earlier or later time, mention it when you book — the property can't guarantee it, but will try to accommodate you. If you're arriving outside reception hours, call ahead on 02 6944 0300 to arrange access.

Do any rooms have a sea view?

No — Tuckerbox Motor Inn is an inland highway motel in South Gundagai, NSW, with no sea views.

What's included in every room?

Every room has a kitchenette, free WiFi, a tea/coffee maker, towels, an iron and board, an in-room heater and toilet amenities. Exact bed configurations and extra features vary by room type — see Rooms & accommodation for all six.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and American Express.

How do I check availability or make a booking?

Use the online booking system for live rates and availability, or call reception directly on 02 6944 0300.

These answers come from the motel's own official site. A couple of other visitor questions there had answers we couldn't fully reconcile with the rest of the property's published information, so rather than repeat something possibly outdated, we've left them out here and flagged them for the owner to confirm.
